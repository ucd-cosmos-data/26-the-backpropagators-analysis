## Literature Evidence

### Platelet factor 4(PF4) and its multiple roles in diseases.

- **Journal:** Blood reviews
- **Year:** 2024
- **PMID:** [38008700](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38008700/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.blre.2023.101155
- **Study type:** Review
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Platelet factor 4 (PF4) combines with heparin to form an antigen that could produce IgG antibodies and participate in heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT).

### Proteomic screening identifies PF4/Cxcl4 as a critical driver of myelofibrosis.

- **Journal:** Leukemia
- **Year:** 2024
- **PMID:** [39025985](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39025985/)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41375-024-02354-z
- **Study type:** Human and laboratory/animal study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: The chemokine Platelet Factor 4 (PF4)/Cxcl4 was up-regulated in all proteomes and increased in plasma and BM fluids of fibrotic mice.

### Anti-PF4 disorders: Pathogenesis, diagnosis and treatment.

- **Journal:** British journal of haematology
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [40589323](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40589323/)
- **DOI:** 10.1111/bjh.20216
- **Study type:** Review
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Platelet factor 4 (PF4) is a cationic protein, able to form complexes with negatively charged molecules upon its self-assembly into PF4 tetramers.
