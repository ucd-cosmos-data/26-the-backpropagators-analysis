## Biological Function

- **Official gene name:** perforin 1
- **Immune function:** Pore-forming cytotoxic-granule protein required for normal killing by NK cells and cytotoxic T cells.
- **Common immune-cell contexts:** NK cells; cytotoxic CD8 T cells
- **Reported biological role:** Enables cytotoxic granule contents to act on target cells; pathogenic variants can impair lymphocyte cytotoxicity.

## Literature Evidence

- **Journal:** British journal of haematology
- **Year:** 2002
- **PMID:** [12060139](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12060139/)
