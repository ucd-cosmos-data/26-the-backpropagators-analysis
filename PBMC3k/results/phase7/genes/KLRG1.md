- **Year:** 2025

### KLRG1 identifies regulatory T cells with mitochondrial alterations that accumulate with aging.

- **Journal:** Nature aging
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [40307497](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40307497/)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s43587-025-00855-9
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Our analysis identifies a subpopulation of regulatory T (Treg) cells that is characterized by the extracellular expression of the co-inhibitory molecule killer cell lectin-like receptor subfamily G member 1 (KLRG1) and accumulates with aging in humans and mice.
