## Literature Evidence

### CD3 deficiencies.

- **Journal:** Current opinion in allergy and clinical immunology
- **Year:** 2005
- **PMID:** [16264327](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16264327/)
- **DOI:** 10.1097/01.all.0000191886.12645.79
- **Study type:** Review
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: RECENT FINDINGS: Homozygous mutations in CD3D and CD3E genes lead to a complete block in T-cell development and thus to an early-onset severe combined immunodeficiency phenotype.

### Concomitant overexpression of mir-182-5p and mir-182-3p raises the possibility of IL-17-producing Treg formation in breast cancer by targeting CD3d, ITK, FOXO1, and NFATs: A meta-analysis and experimental study.

- **Journal:** Cancer science
- **Year:** 2021
- **PMID:** [33283362](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33283362/)
- **DOI:** 10.1111/cas.14764
- **Study type:** Human laboratory study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: FOXO1, CD3d, ITK, NFATc3, NFATc4, and IL-2RA were targeted by miR-182, due to which their expression decreased in PBMCs of patients.

### CD3D: a prognostic biomarker associated with immune infiltration and immunotherapeutic response in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma.

- **Journal:** Bioengineered
- **Year:** 2022
- **PMID:** [35712757](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35712757/)
- **DOI:** 10.1080/21655979.2022.2084254
- **Study type:** Human study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Recent studies have demonstrated that CD3D activates T-cell-related signal transduction and is associated with the antitumor immune response in several cancers.
