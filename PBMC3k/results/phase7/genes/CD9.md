## Literature Evidence

### Distinctive CD39+CD9+ lung interstitial macrophages suppress IL-23/Th17-mediated neutrophilic asthma by inhibiting NETosis.

- **Journal:** Nature communications
- **Year:** 2024
- **PMID:** [39366998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39366998/)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41467-024-53038-2
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: In our neutrophil-dominant asthma (NDA) model, single-cell RNA-seq analysis identifies a subpopulation of CD39+CD9+ interstitial macrophages (IMs) suppressed by IL-23 in NDA conditions but increased by an IL-23 inhibitor αIL-23p19.

### CCN5 suppresses injury-induced vascular restenosis by inhibiting smooth muscle cell proliferation and facilitating endothelial repair via thymosin β4 and Cd9 pathway.

- **Journal:** European heart journal
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [39873228](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39873228/)
- **DOI:** 10.1093/eurheartj/ehae911
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Also, CCN5rp promoted EC repair to suppress neointimal hyperplasia via interaction with Cd9 extracellular domain.

### Diversity and immune dynamics of choroid plexus macrophages are shaped by distinct developmental origins.

- **Journal:** Nature neuroscience
- **Year:** 2026
- **PMID:** [41566006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41566006/)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41593-025-02158-z
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Using single-cell transcriptomics combined with lineage and spatial tracing methods, we identified three biologically distinct populations of choroid plexus macrophages, defined by differential expression of CD163, MHCII or CD9.
