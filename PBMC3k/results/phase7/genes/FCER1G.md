## Literature Evidence

### Fc Fragment of IgE Receptor Ig (FCER1G) acts as a key gene involved in cancer immune infiltration and tumour microenvironment.

- **Journal:** Immunology
- **Year:** 2023
- **PMID:** [36054819](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36054819/)
- **DOI:** 10.1111/imm.13557
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Although recent studies have revealed the relationship between Fc Fragment of IgE Receptor Ig (FCER1G) and human tumours, there is still a lack of a more comprehensive pan-cancer analysis of FCER1G as an immune-related gene.

### Peripheral immune mapping and multi-omics analysis in Pd-1 inhibitor-induced myocarditis.

- **Year:** 2023
- **PMID:** [37202883](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37202883/)
