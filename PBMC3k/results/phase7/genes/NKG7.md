## Biological Function

- **Official gene name:** natural killer cell granule protein 7
- **Immune function:** Granule-associated protein studied as a regulator of cytotoxic granule release in NK and T cells.
- **Common immune-cell contexts:** NK cells; cytotoxic CD8 T cells; selected activated CD4 T-cell states
- **Reported biological role:** Experimental studies report effects on granule exocytosis, target-cell killing, and downstream inflammation.
