### New Insights of CCR7 Signaling in Dendritic Cell Migration and Inflammatory Diseases.

- **Journal:** Frontiers in pharmacology
- **Year:** 2022
- **PMID:** [35281921](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35281921/)
- **DOI:** 10.3389/fphar.2022.841687

- **Year:** 2026
- **PMID:** [40513067](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40513067/)
