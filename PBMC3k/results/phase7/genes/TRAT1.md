## Literature Evidence

### The Roles and Mechanisms of TRAT1 in the Progression of Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer.

- **Journal:** Current medical science
- **Year:** 2022
- **PMID:** [36184729](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36184729/)
- **DOI:** 10.1007/s11596-022-2625-1
- **Study type:** Human study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: OBJECTIVE: T cell receptor-associated transmembrane adaptor 1 (TRAT1) is one of the hub genes regulating T cell receptors (TCRs).

### T cell receptor associated transmembrane adaptor 1 (TRAT1) modulates human Th17 and Treg responses via PI3-kinase and STAT dependent mechanisms.

- **Journal:** Cell communication and signaling : CCS
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [41074026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41074026/)
- **DOI:** 10.1186/s12964-025-02429-z
- **Study type:** Human laboratory study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: T cell Receptor Associated Transmembrane Adaptor 1 (TRAT1) has been implicated in modulating TCR complex stability, but its functional role in human effector and regulatory CD4⁺ T cell subsets remains poorly understood.

### The BET degrader BETd-246 demonstrated significant anti-tumor efficacy in T-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia by inhibiting TRAT1.

- **Journal:** Experimental cell research
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [41076046](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41076046/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.yexcr.2025.114791
- **Study type:** Human and laboratory/animal study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: RNA sequencing analysis indicated significant downregulation of T-cell receptor (TCR)-associated transmembrane adaptor 1 (TRAT1) expression in T-ALL cells treated with BETd-246.
