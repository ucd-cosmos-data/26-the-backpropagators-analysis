## Biological Function

- **Official gene name:** cathepsin W
- **Immune function:** Lysosomal cysteine protease enriched in NK cells and cytotoxic T cells; its indispensable substrates and mechanism remain uncertain.
- **Common immune-cell contexts:** NK cells; cytotoxic CD8 T cells
- **Reported biological role:** Human cell studies report cytotoxic-lymphocyte expression and secretion during target contact, while knockout evidence questions whether it is essential for killing.

## Literature Evidence

### Cathepsin W expressed exclusively in CD8+ T cells and NK cells, is secreted during target cell killing but is not essential for cytotoxicity in human CTLs.

- **Journal:** Experimental hematology
- **Year:** 2009
- **PMID:** [19100676](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19100676/)

- **Study type:** Human laboratory study

## Limitations
