## Literature Evidence

### Modular genome-wide gene expression architecture shared by early traits of osteoporosis and atherosclerosis in the Young Finns Study.

- **Journal:** Scientific reports
- **Year:** 2021
- **PMID:** [33782480](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33782480/)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41598-021-86536-0
- **Study type:** Human study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: The three most significant member genes from the significant modules were NOSIP, GXYLT2, and TRIM63 (p.adj ≤ 0.18).

### Peptide vaccine-treated, long-term surviving cancer patients harbor self-renewing tumor-specific CD8+ T cells.

- **Journal:** Nature communications
- **Year:** 2022
- **PMID:** [35660746](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35660746/)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41467-022-30861-z
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Tracking TCR clonotypes at the single cell level reveals in two patients that peptide-specific long-lasting CD8+ T cells acquire an effector memory phenotype that associates with cell cycle-related genes (CCNA2 and CDK1), and are characterized by high expression of IL7R, SELL, and NOSIP along with a later stage promotion of the AP-1 transcription factor network (5 years or more past vaccination).

### NOSIP overexpression promotes long-term persistence of CD8+ T cells during chronic infection.

- **Journal:** Frontiers in immunology
- **Year:** 2026
- **PMID:** [42440569](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42440569/)
- **DOI:** 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1755657
- **Study type:** Laboratory/animal study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: In this study, we demonstrate that overexpression of nitric oxide synthase-interacting protein (NOSIP) enhances the persistence of antigen-specific CD8+ T cells under chronic antigen stimulation.
