## Literature Evidence

### Lrp10 suppresses IL7R limiting CD8 T cell homeostatic expansion and anti-tumor immunity.

- **Journal:** EMBO reports
- **Year:** 2024
- **PMID:** [38956225](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38956225/)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s44319-024-00191-w
- **Study type:** Human and laboratory/animal study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: T-cell activation induces Lrp10 expression, which post-translationally suppresses IL7 receptor (IL7R) levels.

### Single-Cell Transcriptomic Profile of Innate Cell Populations in Mesenteric Lymph Nodes of Inflammatory Bowel Disease Patients.

- **Journal:** Inflammatory bowel diseases
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [39982469](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39982469/)
- **DOI:** 10.1093/ibd/izaf017

- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Among NK/ILC clusters, we identified a cytotoxic ILC subset (IL7R, KLRD1, GNLY), previously not reported in MLNs, reminiscent of cytotoxic ILC1-like cells found in inflamed gut mucosa.
