## Literature Evidence

### Immune system disturbances in schizophrenia.

- **Journal:** Biological psychiatry
- **Year:** 2014
- **PMID:** [23890736](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23890736/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.biopsych.2013.06.010
- **Study type:** Review
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Recent data suggest that IFITM3 expression is a critical mediator of maternal immune activation.

### IFITM3 enhances immunosensitivity via MHC-I regulation and is associated with the efficacy of anti-PD-1/-L1 therapy in SCLC.

- **Journal:** Molecular cancer
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [40611157](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40611157/)
- **DOI:** 10.1186/s12943-025-02383-x
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Clinical data from SCLC patients treated with PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors were used to investigate the associations between treatment efficacy and IFITM3 expression.

### Periodontitis-induced neuroinflammation triggers IFITM3-Aβ axis to cause alzheimer's disease-like pathology and cognitive decline.

- **Journal:** Alzheimer's research & therapy
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [40684176](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40684176/)
- **DOI:** 10.1186/s13195-025-01818-3
- **Study type:** Laboratory/animal study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Recently, interferon-induced transmembrane protein 3 (IFITM3), an inflammation-induced innate immunity protein, was identified as a novel γ-secretase modulatory protein for Aβ production in AD.
