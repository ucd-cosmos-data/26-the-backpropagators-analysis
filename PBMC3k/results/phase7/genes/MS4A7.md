## Literature Evidence

### The short isoform of MS4A7 is a novel player in glioblastoma microenvironment, M2 macrophage polarization, and tumor progression.

- **Journal:** Journal of neuroinflammation
- **Year:** 2023
- **PMID:** [36944954](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36944954/)
- **DOI:** 10.1186/s12974-023-02766-1
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: The short isoform of MS4A7 (MS4A7-s) was selected for evaluation by RT-PCR and western blotting in clinical specimens.

### Hepatic danger signaling triggers TREM2+ macrophage induction and drives steatohepatitis via MS4A7-dependent inflammasome activation.

- **Journal:** Science translational medicine
- **Year:** 2024
- **PMID:** [38478630](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38478630/)
- **DOI:** 10.1126/scitranslmed.adk1866
- **Study type:** Human and laboratory/animal study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Here, we identify membrane-spanning 4-domains a7 (MS4A7) as a NAM-specific pathogenic factor that exacerbates MASH progression in mice.

### Lipid droplet efferocytosis attenuates proinflammatory signaling in macrophages via TREM2- and MS4A7-dependent mechanisms.

- **Journal:** Cell reports
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [39954254](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39954254/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.celrep.2025.115310
- **Study type:** Human and laboratory/animal study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Additionally, MS4A7 downregulation contributes to LD efferocytosis-mediated dampening of inflammatory response.
