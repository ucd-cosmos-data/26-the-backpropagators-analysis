## Literature Evidence

### CCL5/CCR5 axis in human diseases and related treatments.

- **Journal:** Genes & diseases
- **Year:** 2022
- **PMID:** [34514075](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34514075/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.gendis.2021.08.004
- **Study type:** Review
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: CCL5/CCR5 combination is known for facilitating inflammatory responses, as well as inducing the adhesion and migration of different T cell subsets in immune responses.

### A new integrative analysis of histopathology and single cell RNA-seq reveals the CCL5 mediated T and NK cell interaction with vascular cells in idiopathic pulmonary arterial hypertension.

- **Journal:** Journal of translational medicine
- **Year:** 2024
- **PMID:** [38797830](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38797830/)
- **DOI:** 10.1186/s12967-024-05304-6
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: RESULTS: Through an extensive bioinformatics analysis, CXCL9, CCL5, GZMA and GZMK were identified as hub genes that distinguished IPAH patients from controls.

### Astrocyte-derived CCL5-mediated CCR5+ neutrophil infiltration drives depression pathogenesis.

- **Journal:** Science advances
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [40397747](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40397747/)
- **DOI:** 10.1126/sciadv.adt6632
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Furthermore, by genetic or pharmacologic disruption, we identified a chemotactic effect of the astrocyte-derived chemokine CCL5 on mediating the infiltration of CCR5+ neutrophils and behavioral disorders in male depressed mice.

## Limitations

- Association is not diagnosis and does not establish causality.
- Gene expression is context-dependent and can change with cell state or stimulation.
- Evidence from tumors, infected tissue, animal models, or cell lines may not transfer to peripheral blood.
- Cell-type and tissue specificity limit generalization.
- A marker can identify a cell state without being the mechanism that creates that state.
- This is a focused evidence set, not a formal systematic review or meta-analysis.
