## Literature Evidence

### Sparcl1 and Atherosclerosis.

- **Journal:** Journal of inflammation research
- **Year:** 2023
- **PMID:** [37220502](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37220502/)
- **DOI:** 10.2147/JIR.S406907
- **Study type:** Review
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Sparcl-1 is a cysteine-rich secretory stromal cell protein present in the extracellular matrix and belongs to the Sparc family of proteins.

### Single-cell transcriptomics reveals novel chondrocyte and osteoblast subtypes and their role in knee osteoarthritis pathogenesis.

- **Journal:** Signal transduction and targeted therapy
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [39904988](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39904988/)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41392-025-02136-8
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Sparc+ osteoblasts have negatively regulated bone mineralization and osteoblastic differentiation, aggravated the pathological remodeling of subchondral bone, and promoted the progression of KOA.

### Single-cell RNA sequencing-guided engineering of mitochondrial therapies for intervertebral disc degeneration by regulating mtDNA/SPARC-STING signaling.

- **Journal:** Bioactive materials
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [40104024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40104024/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.bioactmat.2025.02.036
- **Study type:** Other PubMed-indexed study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: The obtained multi-bioactive biotherapy exhibited significantly enhanced benefits in IVDD treatment, in terms of reversing IVDD progression and restoring structural integrity through the mtDNA/SPARC-STING signaling pathways.
