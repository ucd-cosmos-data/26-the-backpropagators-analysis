## Literature Evidence

### Comprehensive Profiling of an Aging Immune System Reveals Clonal GZMK+ CD8+ T Cells as Conserved Hallmark of Inflammaging.

- **Journal:** Immunity
- **Year:** 2021
- **PMID:** [33271118](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33271118/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.immuni.2020.11.005
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: We defined organ-specific and common immune alterations and identified a subpopulation of age-associated granzyme K (GZMK)-expressing CD8+ T (Taa) cells that are distinct from T effector memory (Tem) cells.

### GZMK-expressing CD8+ T cells promote recurrent airway inflammatory diseases.

- **Journal:** Nature
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [39814882](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39814882/)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41586-024-08395-9
- **Study type:** Human and laboratory/animal study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: By comparing T cell repertoires in nasal polyp tissues obtained from consecutive surgeries, here we report that persistent CD8+ T cell clones carrying effector memory-like features colonize the mucosal tissue during disease recurrence, and these cells characteristically express the tryptase Granzyme K (GZMK).

### GZMK+CD8+ T cells: multifaceted roles beyond cytotoxicity.

- **Journal:** Trends in immunology
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [40651883](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40651883/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.it.2025.06.003
- **Study type:** Review
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Among these, granzyme-K-expressing (GZMK+) CD8+ T cells represent a distinct subset characterized by low cytotoxicity but heightened proinflammatory activity, by contrast with their granzyme-B-expressing (GZMB+) counterparts with high cytotoxicity.
