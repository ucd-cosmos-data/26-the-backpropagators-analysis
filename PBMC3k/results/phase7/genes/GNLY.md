## Biological Function

- **Official gene name:** granulysin
- **Immune function:** Cytotoxic and antimicrobial granule protein reported in human NK cells and cytotoxic T cells.
- **Common immune-cell contexts:** NK cells; cytotoxic CD8 T cells; gamma-delta T cells
- **Reported biological role:** Reported to damage microbial or target-cell membranes, with precursor and mature forms stored in distinct effector-vesicle contexts.

## Literature Evidence

### Granulysin.

- **Journal:** Current opinion in immunology
- **Year:** 2003
- **PMID:** [14499265](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14499265/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/s0952-7915(03)00097-9
- **Study type:** Review
- **Organizing summary:** Review summarizes granulysin structure, processing, antimicrobial activity, and cytotoxic activity.

### Granulysin species segregate to different lysosome-related effector vesicles (LREV) and get mobilized by either classical or non-classical degranulation.

- **Journal:** Molecular immunology
- **Year:** 2019
- **PMID:** [30658247](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30658247/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.molimm.2018.12.031
- **Study type:** Human laboratory study
- **Organizing summary:** Human lymphocyte experiments report that granulysin forms occupy different effector vesicles and can follow different release routes.

### Decidual NK Cells Transfer Granulysin to Selectively Kill Bacteria in Trophoblasts.

- **Journal:** Cell
- **Year:** 2020
- **PMID:** [32822574](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32822574/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.cell.2020.07.019
- **Study type:** Human ex vivo/laboratory study
- **Organizing summary:** Human decidual and peripheral NK-cell experiments report granulysin transfer that kills intracellular bacteria while sparing host cells.
