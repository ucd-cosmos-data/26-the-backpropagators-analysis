## Literature Evidence

### The CD2-LFA-3 and LFA-1-ICAM pathways: relevance to T-cell recognition.

- **Journal:** Immunology today
- **Year:** 1989
- **PMID:** [2482743](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2482743/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/0167-5699(89)90039-X
- **Study type:** Review
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: In this review Malegapuru Makgoba, Martin Sanders and Stephen Shaw focus primarily on the two molecular pathways of lymphocyte adhesion that have been shown to play a critical role in facilitation of antigen-specific recognition, namely CD2 and its ligand, lymphocyte function associated antigen-3 (LFA-3), and LFA-1 and its ligand, intercellular adhesion molecule-1 (ICAM-1).

### T cell adhesion, avidity regulation and signaling: a molecular analysis of CD2.

- **Journal:** Seminars in immunology
- **Year:** 1993
- **PMID:** [7693022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7693022/)
- **DOI:** 10.1006/smim.1993.1029
- **Study type:** Review
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: One such co-receptor molecule is CD2, a T cell glycoprotein that not only participates in T cell activation but also provides the T cell with a major adhesion pathway whose avidity is regulated by TcR triggering.

### An "off-the-shelf" CD2 universal CAR-T therapy for T-cell malignancies.

- **Journal:** Leukemia
- **Year:** 2023
- **PMID:** [37798328](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37798328/)
- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41375-023-02039-z
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: To broaden the use of CAR-T cells in pan T-cell malignancies, we developed an allogeneic "universal" CD2-targeting CAR-T cell (UCART2), in which the CD2 antigen is deleted to prevent fratricide, and the T-cell receptor is removed to prevent GvHD.
