## Biological Function

- **Official gene name:** granzyme A
- **Immune function:** Cytotoxic-lymphocyte serine protease with reported inflammatory and target-cell death activities that differ from granzyme B.
- **Common immune-cell contexts:** NK cells; cytotoxic CD8 T cells
- **Reported biological role:** Granule-released protease; selected studies report context-dependent substrate cleavage and inflammatory or cell-death outcomes.

## Limitations

- Evidence from tumors, infected tissue, animal models, or cell lines may not transfer to healthy peripheral blood.

- This is a focused evidence set, not a formal systematic review or meta-analysis.

## Plain Language Notes

GZMA makes granzyme A, another enzyme carried by killer immune cells; it does not always act in the same way as granzyme B.

Finding this gene in the PBMC3k NK cluster helps describe the cells. By itself, it cannot reveal a person's health, identity, or future.
