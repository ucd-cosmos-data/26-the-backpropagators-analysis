## Literature Evidence

### FCER2 (CD23) asthma-related single nucleotide polymorphisms yields increased IgE binding and Egr-1 expression in human B cells.

- **Journal:** American journal of respiratory cell and molecular biology
- **Year:** 2014
- **PMID:** [24010859](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24010859/)
- **DOI:** 10.1165/rcmb.2013-0112OC
- **Study type:** Human laboratory study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Polymorphisms within FCER2, the gene encoding CD23, have been associated with atopy, increased risk of exacerbations in patients with asthma, and high serum IgE levels.

### Correlations between exhaled nitric oxide, rs28364072 polymorphism of FCER2 gene, asthma control, and inhaled corticosteroid responsiveness in children with asthma.

- **Journal:** Journal of breath research
- **Year:** 2020
- **PMID:** [33108776](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33108776/)
- **DOI:** 10.1088/1752-7163/abc4ec
- **Study type:** Human study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: This study aimed to describe the clinical and biological characteristics, and its correlation with polymorphism of rs28364072 in FCER2 of asthmatic children.

### IgG memory B cells expressing IL4R and FCER2 are associated with atopic diseases.

- **Journal:** Allergy
- **Year:** 2023
- **PMID:** [36445014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36445014/)
- **DOI:** 10.1111/all.15601
- **Study type:** Human single-cell/transcriptomic study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: RESULTS: We identified a novel population of IgG memory B cells characterized by the expression of IL-4/IL-13 regulated genes FCER2/CD23, IL4R, IL13RA1, and IGHE, denoting a history of differentiation during type 2 immune responses.
