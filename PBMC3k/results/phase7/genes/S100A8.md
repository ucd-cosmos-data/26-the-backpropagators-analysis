### S100A8/A9: From basic science to clinical application.

### Alarmin S100A8 imparts chemoresistance of esophageal cancer by reprogramming cancer-associated fibroblasts.

- **Journal:** Cell reports. Medicine
- **Year:** 2024
- **PMID:** [38776909](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38776909/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.xcrm.2024.101576
- **Study type:** Human and laboratory/animal study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Mechanistically, we reveal that cancer-cell-derived S100A8 triggers the intracellular RhoA-ROCK-MLC2-MRTF-A pathway by binding to the CD147 receptor of CAFs, inducing CAF polarization and leading to chemoresistance.

### Inhibition of S100A8/A9 ameliorates neuroinflammation by blocking NET formation following traumatic brain injury.

- **Journal:** Redox biology
- **Year:** 2025
- **PMID:** [39929053](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39929053/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.redox.2025.103532
- **Study type:** Human and laboratory/animal study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: S100A8/A9, also known as calprotectin or myeloid-related protein-8/14 (MRP8/14), is an alarmin primarily secreted by activated neutrophils with potent pro-inflammatory property.
