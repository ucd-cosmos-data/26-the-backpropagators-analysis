## Literature Evidence

### New insights into 5q- syndrome as a ribosomopathy.

- **Journal:** Cell cycle (Georgetown, Tex.)
- **Year:** 2010
- **PMID:** [20980806](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20980806/)
- **DOI:** 10.4161/cc.9.21.13742
- **Study type:** Review
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: Recently, two novel mouse models have provided evidence for the involvement of both RPS14 and the p53 pathway, and specific miRNAs in 5q- syndrome.

### The effect of miR-223 on cellular behaviour in non-5q myelodysplastic syndromes through targeting RPS14.

- **Journal:** Pathology
- **Year:** 2020
- **PMID:** [32571542](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32571542/)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.pathol.2020.03.010
- **Study type:** Human laboratory study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: A decrease in RPS14 expression in non-5q MDS patients was confirmed by immunohistochemical analyses of MDS bone marrow biopsies.

### TLR7 ligation augments hematopoiesis in Rps14 (uS11) deficiency via paradoxical suppression of inflammatory signaling.

- **Journal:** Blood advances
- **Year:** 2021
- **PMID:** [34432872](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34432872/)
- **DOI:** 10.1182/bloodadvances.2020003055
- **Study type:** Human and laboratory/animal study
- **Organizing summary:** PubMed abstract evidence: We developed a model of MDS in zebrafish with knockout of Rps14, the primary mediator of the anemia associated with del(5q) MDS.
